The Oswego church supper, 1940
==============================

Source:   Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Epidemiology
          Program Office. Oswego: An Outbreak of Gastrointestinal
          Illness Following a Church Supper. Case Studies in Applied
          Epidemiology, No. 401-303, Student's Guide. The guide credits
          updates and editing to Philip Brachman, Michael Gregg, Richard
          Dicker and others.
          https://www.cdc.gov/eis/downloads/xoswego-401-303-student.pdf

History:  Gross M. Oswego County revisited. Public Health Reports,
          March-April 1976, 168-170.
          https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/65198/cdc_65198_DS1.pdf

Terms:    The guide is a publication of a United States government
          agency and carries no copyright notice that was found. Works
          of the United States government are generally in the public
          domain. No explicit statement of that was read for this
          document, so this is a reasonable reading and not a confirmed
          fact.

Checked:  Compared with the oswego data in the R package epitools
          0.5-10.1, licensed GPL (>= 2). No epitools file is
          redistributed here. The values come from CDC's table.
